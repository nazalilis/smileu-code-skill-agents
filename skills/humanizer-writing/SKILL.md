# Humanizer Writing Skill

Inspired by `blader/humanizer`.

Large language models are statistically biased toward generic, melodramatic, and repetitive sentence patterns. This skill strips out synthetic tells so that writing, code comments, commit logs, and documentation sound like they were crafted by a thoughtful human engineer.

## The 25 Anti-AI Patterns

### 1. Staging Instead of Stating
- ❌ **"Not X, but Y":** *"It's not just a tool, it's a revolution."* → State the claim directly: *"It automates deployments."*
- ❌ **Dramatic one-liners:** *"That is the real win."*, *"And the rest is history."* → Cut or integrate into facts.
- ❌ **Deep-sounding clichés:** *"At its core, what truly matters is..."*, *"Simplicity is the language of mastery."* → State the concrete fact.
- ❌ **Fake run-ups:** *"Let's dive in!"*, *"Honestly? It depends."*, *"Here's the thing..."* → Cut the warm-up, begin with the point.
- ❌ **Arguing with ghosts:** *"One might be tempted to think..."* → Remove fake counter-arguments.

### 2. Rhythm by Rule
- ❌ **Forced Triads:** *"efficiency, agility, and resilience"* → Use the exact number of words meaning demands.
- ❌ **Repeated Openings:** Three consecutive sentences starting with *"This allows..."* or *"By doing X..."* → Vary sentence structure.
- ❌ **Dashes Everywhere:** Overuse of em-dashes (`—`) to glue disparate thoughts → Use standard periods, commas, or semicolons.

### 3. Overused AI Vocabulary (The Banned List)
Replace these words with simple, honest equivalents:
- *delve* → explore, look at, examine
- *testament* → proof, sign, result
- *pivotal / tapestry / landscape / realm* → state what it actually is
- *showcasing / boasts / features* → has, includes, shows
- *nestled within / breathtaking* → located in

### 4. Formatting Clutter
- ❌ **Unnecessary Bold:** Randomly bolding every second keyword in a paragraph. Bold only headers or critical warnings.
- ❌ **Emoji Overload:** Decorative emojis on every single bullet point (🚀, 💡, 🔥, ✨). Use clean standard markdown.
- ❌ **Chatbot Residue:** *"Certainly! Here is your requested code:"*, *"I hope this helps! Feel free to ask if you have more questions."* → Remove entirely.
